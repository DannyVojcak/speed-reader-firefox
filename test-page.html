Welcome to the Speed Reader Test Page

This is a test page designed to demonstrate the Speed Reader Firefox extension. The extension allows you to read web content at high speeds by presenting text one word at a time, similar to Spritz Technology.

How to Use the Extension

To test the Speed Reader extension:

  1. Install the extension in Firefox
  2. Click the Speed Reader icon in your toolbar
  3. Adjust the reading speed and appearance settings
  4. Click "Start Speed Reading" to begin
  5. Click anywhere on the reading screen to stop

About Speed Reading

Speed reading is a technique that allows you to read and understand text at a much faster rate than normal reading. The Speed Reader extension implements a method similar to Spritz Technology, which presents words one at a time with a focus point to guide your eye movement.

The key to effective speed reading is reducing the number of eye movements required to read text. By presenting one word at a time with a consistent focus point, your eyes don't need to move across the page, allowing you to read much faster while maintaining comprehension.

Features of This Extension

  • Adjustable Speed: Control reading speed from 100 to 1000 words per minute
  • Customizable Appearance: Change font size, colors, and focus point
  • Easy Controls: Simple popup interface with intuitive settings
  • Click to Stop: Click anywhere on the reading screen to stop
  • Settings Persistence: Your preferences are saved automatically

Tips for Effective Speed Reading

Here are some tips to get the most out of the Speed Reader extension:

  1. Start Slow: Begin with a comfortable speed (200-300 WPM) and gradually increase
  2. Focus on the Red Dot: Keep your eyes focused on the red focus point
  3. Don't Subvocalize: Try to avoid "hearing" the words in your head
  4. Practice Regularly: Like any skill, speed reading improves with practice
  5. Adjust Settings: Find the font size and colors that work best for you

Remember that speed reading is a skill that takes time to develop. Don't be discouraged if you don't see immediate results. With regular practice, you'll be able to read much faster while maintaining good comprehension.
